How this forecast is made

Sources: AEMO NEMWeb 5-minute dispatch (DispatchIS) and pre-dispatch (PredispatchIS) for the NSW1 region today, AEMO aggregated price and demand files for earlier days, and the Open-Meteo forecast for Sydney temperature.

AEMO's daily file only runs to the previous midnight. To forecast tomorrow, today is filled with dispatch actuals so far and AEMO pre-dispatch for the rest of the day. The models then train on the past 365 days and forecast all 48 periods of tomorrow.

Hybrid is the project model: yesterday's profile (seasonal naive) plus an LSTM correction learned from temperature and calendar effects. ARIMA and naive are the baselines. AEMO pre-dispatch is the market operator's own schedule, built from generator bids, and is the benchmark to beat.

On live data since July 2026 the naive baseline has been as accurate as the hybrid, so read the spread between the lines as the uncertainty. Price spikes are not predictable from history alone.
